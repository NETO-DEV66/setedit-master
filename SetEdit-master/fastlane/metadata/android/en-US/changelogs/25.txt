Privileged access is now granted once and remembered.

* Connect once, stay connected. After a single wireless-debugging (or
  Shizuku, or root) connection, SetEdit grants itself every permission it
  needs and never asks you to connect again. The remembered ADB endpoint
  is reused, so reconnects skip the slow mDNS discovery that used to time
  out and send you back to the pairing dialog.
* No more repeated prompts in the Apps screen. Tapping any action used to
  show "grant privileged access first"; now the app silently restores
  access — root, a Shizuku grant, or a reconnect — and only opens the
  connect screen when the device has genuinely never had access.
* Shizuku is actually requested. The app previously only checked whether
  Shizuku was already approved, so a device running Shizuku still asked
  for wireless debugging. It now asks Shizuku for permission directly.
* One grant covers everything. A single connection grants
  WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS, WRITE_SETTINGS and usage access, and sets the
  matching AppOps, so settings edits and the Apps Manager work without the
  connection.
* Survives a WiFi drop. A failed command reconnects from the remembered
  endpoint and retries once, instead of failing until you reconnect by hand.
