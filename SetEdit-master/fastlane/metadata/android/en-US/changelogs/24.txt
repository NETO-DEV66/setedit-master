The Apps Manager — manage the other apps on your phone, all in one place.

* A new Apps screen. Browse every app installed on the device, with its
  icon, package name, size and a System badge. Search by name or package,
  and filter to user or system apps.
* Per-app actions. Open an app, force stop it, jump to its system info
  page, clear its cache, or clear all its data. Disable or enable it, and
  uninstall it. System apps are removed for the current user only, so the
  APK stays safe in /system.
* Freeze and unfreeze. Suspend an app so it stops running in the
  background without losing its data.
* Permissions, in one list. See the runtime permissions an app asks for
  and grant or revoke any of them.
* AppOps switches. Flip hidden switches such as WAKE_LOCK,
  RUN_IN_BACKGROUND, POST_NOTIFICATION and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM.
* Battery and standby. Exempt an app from battery optimisation, or move
  it between the Active, Frequent, Rare and Restricted standby buckets.
* Safe by design. Destructive actions ask first, and every shell command
  is validated so a bad package name can never run anything unexpected.
