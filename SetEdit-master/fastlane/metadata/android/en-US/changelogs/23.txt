A big one — sort, history, widgets and smarter recipes.

* Sort any table. Order a list by name or by value, A-Z or Z-A, straight
  from the toolbar.
* Change history. Every edit is recorded, newest first. Open the history
  and tap an entry to put the old value back.
* Copy anything. Long-press a row, or use the editor, to copy a key, a
  value or a whole row to the clipboard.
* Search values too. A new switch in search also matches setting values,
  not just key names.
* Sensitive keys stand out. Risky keys now show a small warning badge in
  the list and inside the editor, so you know before you touch them.
* Recipes, organised. Recipes are grouped into Animations, Display, Touch,
  Accessibility and Custom, and you can search them. You can also write
  your own recipes and keep them alongside the built-ins.
* Auto-backup. Turn it on and SetEdit saves a snapshot before changing a
  sensitive key, so you can always get back.
* Quick Settings tile. Flip straight into SetEdit from the notification
  shade.
* Home-screen widget. Pin your starred settings keys to the home screen
  and open them with one tap.
* Reduce motion. A new setting turns off the bouncy screen and list
  animations if you prefer things calm.
