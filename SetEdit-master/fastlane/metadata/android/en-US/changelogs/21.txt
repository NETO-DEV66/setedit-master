SetEdit can now update itself, and the app feels springier.

* In-app updates: check for a new release, download it and install it without
  leaving the app. Open it from Settings, the Home quick actions, or the prompt
  that appears when a new version is found. The daily check can be turned off in
  Settings.
* Smooth bouncy animations across lists, screens, the editor sheet and the
  bottom navigation.
* Faster, smoother tables: the list keeps its scroll position when you return
  from the editor, and starring a key rebinds only that row.
