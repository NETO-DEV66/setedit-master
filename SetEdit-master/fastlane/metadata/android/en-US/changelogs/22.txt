The recipes work, and the shell behaves.

* Recipes now take effect. The animation presets wrote to the wrong settings
  table, so the system ignored them and nothing changed. They now write where
  Android actually reads the values. Every recipe also reads its value back, so
  one that cannot apply says so instead of reporting a false success.
* Back returns to Home. Pressing back from any other tab now takes you to the
  dashboard instead of closing the app. Back from Home still leaves the app.
* Full-screen mode. The app runs edge-to-edge and hides the status and
  navigation bars. Swipe from an edge to bring them back.
